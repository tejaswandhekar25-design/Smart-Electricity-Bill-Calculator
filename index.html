Energy Saving Tips

Smart ways to reduce your electricity bill and save money

💡

Switch to LED Bulbs

LED bulbs use up to 80% less energy than incandescent bulbs and last 25 times longer.

Save up to ₹2,000/year
❄️

AC Temperature at 24°C

Each degree below 24°C increases energy consumption by 6%. Use the timer and sleep mode.

Save up to ₹3,500/year
⭐

Use 5-Star Rated Appliances

BEE 5-star rated appliances consume 25-30% less electricity compared to lower-rated ones.

Save up to ₹4,000/year
🌀

Optimize Fan Usage

Use BLDC fans that consume only 28-35W compared to 75W for conventional fans.

Save up to ₹1,500/year
🔥

Solar Water Heater

Install a solar water heater to eliminate geyser electricity usage, which accounts for ~25% of bills in winter.

Save up to ₹5,000/year
🔌

Unplug Standby Devices

Standby power accounts for 5-10% of total electricity use. Use power strips to easily switch off.

Save up to ₹1,200/year
🧺

Full Load Washing

Run washing machines only on full load. Use cold water settings to save heating energy.

Save up to ₹800/year
❄️

Fridge Maintenance

Keep fridge coils clean, ensure door seals are tight, and set temp to 3-5°C. Don't place hot food inside.

Save up to ₹1,000/year
☀️

Natural Light & Ventilation

Use natural daylight instead of artificial lighting. Open windows for cross-ventilation to reduce AC usage.

Save up to ₹1,500/year
🏠

Go Solar with Net Metering

Install rooftop solar panels. With net metering, you can sell excess power back to the grid and slash your bill.

Save up to ₹15,000/year
